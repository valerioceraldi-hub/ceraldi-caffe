Ceraldi Caffè / Babàmisù

Babàmisù: babà meets tiramisù

A babà filled with mascarpone cream and soaked in coffee liqueur. Two icons of Italian pastry in a single bite, made by us since 2023.

See the menu with pricesGet directions

The babà is the leavened, syrup-soaked cake that Naples made its own. Tiramisù is the spoon dessert of mascarpone and coffee. The Babàmisù brings them together: a babà filled with mascarpone cream and soaked in coffee liqueur.

Since when

We make it ourselves, in-house, since 2023: the year of Ceraldi's 50th anniversary. Ceraldi has been a family café and pastry shop in Naples since 1973. The same year saw the birth of the Segreto amalfitano, a limoncello babà with a heart of lemon chantilly, like that of the Amalfi lemon delizia.

What it is

  • The babà, soft and soaked.
  • The filling: mascarpone cream.
  • The soaking: coffee liqueur, so the dessert contains alcohol.

Allergens: gluten, milk, eggs. For other needs, ask the staff at the counter.

Where and when

The Babàmisù is always available in our display case at Piazza Carità 14, on via Toledo, from 6:30 am to 8:30 pm. The current price is on the menu.
