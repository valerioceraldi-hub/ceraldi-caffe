Caffè Sfogliata: coffee in a chocolate-lined riccio

Hot coffee, cold cinnamon milk foam and a riccio you can eat, lined with dark chocolate.

See the menu with pricesGet directions

The Caffè Sfogliata is one of our special coffees, and it arrives in an unusual way: the coffee is hot, the cinnamon milk foam is cold, and the container is an edible riccio lined with dark chocolate.

What's in it

  • Hot coffee, served inside the riccio.
  • Cold cinnamon milk foam.
  • An edible riccio lined with dark chocolate, to eat after the coffee.

The contrast between hot coffee and cold foam is the heart of the recipe.

Where to find it

The Caffè Sfogliata is available at the counter and at the table at Ceraldi Caffè, Piazza Carità 14, on via Toledo, every day from 6:30 am to 8:30 pm. The price is on the menu. See also our house specialities.
